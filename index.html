Who We Are

Building a sustainable future through food processing and waste-to-energy solutions

GreenCity Enterprises

GreenCity Enterprises is a Cameroonian holding company dedicated to redefining kitchen economics and energy access in developing nations. Through our two divisions – GreenCity Foods and GreenCity Briquettes – we have engineered a closed-loop solution that starts with food and ends with empowerment.

We believe that environmental stewardship and economic growth can go hand in hand. By processing agricultural produce into premium foods and transforming byproducts into clean-burning charcoal briquettes, we create a circular economy that benefits everyone.

Our purpose is to free the women who carry the daily burden of kitchen management, honor the dignity of rural labour, empower urban households, and prove that sustainability is not just about the environment – it is about people.

Meet the Founder

The vision behind GreenCity Enterprises

Kevin Ndanji Mpafe – Founder & President

Kevin Ndanji Mpafe

Founder & President, GreenCity Enterprises

Kevin Ndanji Mpafe is the Founder & President of GreenCity Enterprises (GCE), the holding company behind GreenCity Briquettes (GCB) and GreenCity Foods (GCF). With over 20 years of professional experience in agribusiness, what began as a childhood calling has evolved into a lifelong mission to transform Cameroon's agricultural landscape.

Witnessing the dual crises of agricultural produce waste and the health hazards of traditional cooking fuels, Kevin engineered a closed-loop solution that starts with food and ends with empowerment. His vision is to redefine kitchen economics and energy access in developing nations – freeing the women who carry the daily burden of kitchen management and creating economic opportunities for rural farmers.

"True sustainability is not just about the environment – it is about people."

Kevin's leadership philosophy is grounded in a Graduate Diploma in Human Resource Management, complemented by certifications in Health & Safety (HSE) and security. These credentials have shaped his approach to building highly productive teams, empowering farmers, distributors, and community partners.

Today, GreenCity Enterprises stands as a testament to his belief that environmental stewardship and economic growth can go hand in hand – honoring the dignity of rural labour while empowering urban households across Cameroon.
